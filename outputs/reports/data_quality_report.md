## Price cleaning

- `price` coerced to numeric; rows with missing or non-positive price removed (0 such rows in train).
- **Implausible prices flagged and excluded from analysis**: price < $10 or > $2,000 → **17 listings**.
- **Statistical outliers**: IQR rule (Q1 − 1.5·IQR, Q3 + 1.5·IQR) applied **within each city × room_type segment** → **14,246 listings (~6.8%)** flagged `price_outlier`. These are retained in the data but flagged; plausible premium listings are not removed.
- **Price cap evidence**: 936 listings sit at exactly $1,000 (the observed maximum) — treated as a plausible upper bound / platform cap, not an error.
- 348 listings priced below $20 — mostly shared rooms / private rooms in low-cost markets; retained unless flagged implausible.

## Analysis-ready dataset

`outputs/cleaned/train_analysis.parquet` — 208,839 rows after removing the 17 implausible-price listings. Missing review scores are kept as NaN ("no score available"), not mean-imputed.

## Engineered variables

- `log_price` = ln(price) — used for modelling/skewed analyses
- `host_experience_years` from `host_since` (reference date 2025-01-01)
- `amenities_count` + 12 binary amenity flags parsed from the `{...}` amenities string
- `price_per_person` = price / accommodates
- `neighbourhood` = first sentence of `neighborhood_overview` (free text; see limitations)
